Three rules the contract enforces after every move

The keeper that rebalances cannot withdraw anyone's money - it can only move USDC between the venues above, within these limits. If a move would break a rule, the transaction reverts.

No single venue gets too much

Every venue has its own ceiling as a share of the vault. A venue without a ceiling is closed. Cap values are in the table above.

Money only goes where it can come back today

After a deposit into a venue, that venue must be able to pay back the whole position immediately. A fully borrowed-out pool or a paused market cannot receive funds. And if one venue freezes later, withdrawals are served from the others instead of failing.

What you should know

Not audited yet. The vault is built on the BoringDeFi contracts already running on Ethereum; the Arbitrum guards and the Compound adapter are new. Start small.
